# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project is a small community around one laptop: people share measurements, hardware reports and fixes, and we want everyone to feel welcome doing that.

- Be respectful. No harassment, insults, or discrimination.
- Assume good faith; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Keep reports factual: include what you did, what you saw, and `truesense doctor --json`.
- Safety comes first: do not push others to try risky settings (undervolts, fans off) without the warnings.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or ban people who break these rules. To report a problem, use the private reporting described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or contact the maintainer privately.
